#Thermodynamics 

[[Thermodynamics/Thermodynamics#Work|+]] Constant temperature, reversible expansion of an ideal gas:
$w = -nRT \cdot ln(\frac{V_2}{V_1})$

[[Thermodynamics/Entropy#Entropy and volume|+]] Constant temperature, expansion of an ideal gas:
$\Delta S = nR \cdot ln(\frac{V_2}{V_1})$
$\Delta S = nR \cdot ln(\frac{p_1}{p_2})$

[[Thermodynamics/Thermodynamics#Heat|+]] Constant volume, no non-expansion work:
$\Delta U = q$

[[Thermodynamics/Enthalpy|+]] Constant pressure, no non-expansion work:
$\Delta H = q$

[[Thermodynamics/Thermodynamics#Heat capacity|+]] Ideal gas:
$C_{p} = C_{V} + nR \Leftrightarrow C_{p, \space m} = C_{V, \space m} + R$

[[Thermodynamics/Entropy#Global changes in entropy|+]] Surroundings (any conditions):
$\Delta S_{\text{surr}} = \frac{q_{\text{surr}}}{T}$

[[Thermodynamics/Entropy#Global changes in entropy|+]] Constant temperature & pressure:
$\Delta S_{\text{surr}} = -\frac{\Delta H}{T}, \space T = \text{const}, \space p = \text{const}$

[[Thermodynamics/Entropy#Gibbs free energy|+]] Ideal gas:
$G_m = G_m \degree + RT \cdot ln(\frac{p}{p \degree}), \space p \degree = 1 \space bar$
