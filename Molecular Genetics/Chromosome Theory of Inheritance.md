## Chromosomes

**Haploid cells** - contain unpaired chromosomes; gametes.
**Diploid cells** - contain paired chromosomes; somatic cells.

**Homologous chromosomes (homologs)** - paired-up chromosomes; contain the same genes, even if different alleles.
**Sister chromatids** - identical; after replication.
**Centromere** - where the sister chromatids are attached.

Centromere position:
- **Metacentric** - centrosome in the middle.
- **Acrocentric** - centrosome closer to one side.
- **Telocentric** - centrosome at the end of the chromosome.

> Humans have 46 chromosomes.
> n = 23, 2n = 46

## Mitosis

Generates 2 daughter cells w/ same \# and type of chromosomes as in the parent cell.
Produces diploid cells.

Mitotic cell cycle:
	**G1 phase** - cell growth.
	**S phase** - duplication of chromosomes into sister chromatids.
	**G2 phase** - cell growth, synthesis of proteins required for mitosis.

**G0 phase** - the cell stops dividing entirely; e.g. nature neurons.

#### S-phase of mitosis

**1\. Prophase**
Chromosomes condense & become visible.

**2\. Prometaphase**
Nuclear envelope breaks down - can use the whole cell for replication.
Spindle forms and sister chromatids attach to microtubules from opposite centrosomes.

**3\. Metaphase**
Chromosomes align at the cell equator (metaphase plate).
Cohesins hold sister chromatids.

**4\. Anaphase**
Sister chromatids separate & move to opposite poles.
Separase breaks the cohesins.

**5\. Telophase**
Spindle disappears.
Chromosomes decondense and are enclosed in two nuclei.

**Cytokinesis** - cytoplasm divides & produces 2 daughter cells.

Some cells do multiple rounds of mitosis without cytokinesis.
**Syncytium** - cell w/ several nuclei.

## Meiosis

Generates gametes containing half of the usual \# of chromosomes.
Produces haploid cells.

Chromosomes replicate once (like in mitosis), nuclei divide twice.

#### Meiosis I
**Reductional division** - halves the \# of chromosomes.

Homologous chromosomes can cross over (exchange DNA between themselves).

**1\. Prophase I (same as in [[#Mitosis|mitosis]])**
Chromosomes condense & become visible.

**2\. Prometaphase I (same as in [[#Mitosis|mitosis]])**
Nuclear envelope breaks down - can use the whole cell for replication.
Spindle forms and sister chromatids attach to microtubules from opposite centrosomes.

**3\. Metaphase I**
Chromosomes align at the cell equator (metaphase plate).
Cohesins hold sister chromatids.

**4\. Anaphase I**
Chromosomes separate into chromatids & move to opposite poles.
Separase breaks the cohesins.

**5\. Telophase I (same as in [[#Mitosis|mitosis]])**
Spindle disappears.
Chromosomes decondense & are enclosed in two nuclei.

**Interkinesis** - a type of cytokinesis that happens after meiosis I.

#### Meiosis II
**Equational division** - produces 4 haploid cells.
Entire process like [[#Mitosis|mitosis]], but the sister chromatids do not replicate after separation.

**1\. Prophase II**
Chromosomes condense & become visible.

**2\. Prometaphase II**
Nuclear envelope breaks down, can use the whole cell for replication.
Spindle forms and sister chromatids attach to microtubules from opposite centrosomes.

**3\. Metaphase II**
Chromosomes align at the cell equator **homologous chromosomes side by side**.
Cohesins hold sister chromatids.

**4\. Anaphase II**
Homologous chromosomes separate and move to opposite poles.
Separase breaks the cohesins.

**5\. Telophase II**
Spindle disappears.
Chromosomes decondense and are enclosed in two nuclei.
