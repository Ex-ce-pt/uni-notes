
==to meoisis?==
Genes on homologous chromosomes violate [[Principles of Heredity#Mendel's laws|Mendel's law of independent segregation]].
Genes in **cis configuration** - on the same chromosome; necessarily segregated to the same gamete during meiosis I.
Genes in **trans configuration** - on different chromosomes; necessarily segregated to different gametes during meiosis I.

**Linkage** - 2 or more genes can be located on the same chromosome.
Genes that are (physically) close together tend to be inherited together.

**Linkage group** - group of genes that are all linked together (inherited together).
\# of linkage groups = \# of types of chromosomes of the species
Genes that are on the same chromosome but far apart can still be inherited differently if crossing over happens.

**Recombinant phenotypes** - crossing over in meiosis prophase I causes that.
