
Microbiome - all microbes associated with a higher organism.
**Resident microbiota** - live permanently.
**Transient microbiota** - live temporarily.

Microbes occupy niches - they don't only produce helpful substances but also squeeze the harmful bacteria out.
Microbiota affects non-infectious diseases.

Affecting human microbiota:
- Age
- Food
- Area of the body
- Person-to-person

> Blood is sterile.




Koch's postulates
determining if microorganism is pathogenic

1. microorganism in every sick person but none of the healthy ones
2. can be isolated
3. healthy should have same symptoms when infected as the sick one
4. can be isolated from the new host
