
**Biofilm** - aggregate of microorganisms in extracellular matrix.
Usually multispecies.

**Classical biofilm** - on surfaces.
**Pellicles** - liquid-air.
**Flocculation** - liquid - aggregate formation.

Functions:
* Attachment to surfaces (biotic/abiotic)
- Aggregation
- Mechanical stability
- Protective barrier (resistance to host defenses/tolerance to antimicrobial agents)
- Enzymatic activity (digestion of exogenous macromolecules) + redox reactions
- Nutrient + water accumulation
- Exchange of genetic information ([[Horizontal gene transfer|HGT]])
- Export of waste
- Lower pH
- High oxidative stress - mutations

80% of human infections are biofilm-associated.
Antibiotic resistance spikes - physical protection + low pH + non-dividing cells inside.

Only 10% are cells by mass, everything else - macromolecules:
- Polysaccharides - structure
- Proteins - enzymes + structural
- DNA - structure + HGT
- Lipids - pores & water channels
- Water

## Formation

#### Stage 1 - initial attachment
(seconds)
Reversible
Physical forces, pili, flagella.

#### Stage 2 - irreversible attachment
(seconds-minutes)
Begin to secrete extracellular polymeric substances.

#### Stage 3 - initial maturation
(hours, days)
Growth, cell division.

#### Stage 4 - final maturation
(hours, days)
formation of structures, water channels

#### Stage 5 - dispersion
(days, months)
cells disperse and move to another location

## Regulation

For biofilm formation right genes need to be transcribed at the right time.
Two-component systems are obviously used.
Cell membrane has an **anti-sigma** factor that blocks transcription until it's degraded by an activated enzyme.

**c-di-GMP (pGpG)** - regulatory molecule that activates biofilm formation, toxin production, cell-cell communication etc.
**Diguanylate cyclase** - forms pGpG.
**Phosphodiesterase** - degrades pGpG.

## Quorum sensing

**Quorum sensing** - regulation of genes depending on population density.
Bacteria release signals to the environment to notify others.

|                      | Autoinducer-1 (AI-1) | Autoinducer-2 (AI-2) | Autoinducing peptides (AIPs) |
| -------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Structure            | Short molecules      | Furanosylborate      | Peptides                     |
| Specificity          | Gram$^-$             | Gram$^-$/Gram$^+$    | Gram$^+$                     |
| Communication target | Intraspecies         | Interspecies         | Intraspecies ==?==           |

> AI-2 is one of the only biomolecules that uses boron.

Bacteria start transcribing the biofilm formation genes if there are enough other bacteria present around.

**Quorum quenching** - degradation of QS molecules.
Can be used to hide presence from predators/immune system.
