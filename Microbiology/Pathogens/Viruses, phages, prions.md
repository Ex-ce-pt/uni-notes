
## Viruses

Tiny & acellular.
Obligate intracellular parasites.
Infect all types of cells, but very specialised.
Effects range from asymptomatic to lethal.
Simple DNA/RNA genome.

**Virion** - outer protein shell + genome; viral particle.

**Capsid** - genome + protein coat.
**Capsomeres** - proteins making up the capsid.

**Naked virus/Non-enveloped virus** - genome + capsid.
**Enveloped virus** - lipid layer surrounding the capsid, stolen from the host.

**Biological vector** - carry virus inside, transmit by biting.
**Mechanical vector** - carry virus on outside, transmit by touch.

**Zoonosis** - animal → human.

For genome replication, see [[DNA Replication & Repair#Viral DNA]].

**Bacteriophages** - viruses that infect bacteria only.
Also see [[Horizontal gene transfer#Transduction]].

**Latent infection** - stays dormant.
Can be either inside or outside of the host's genome.
**Chronic infection** - recurrent/persistent over time.

#### HIV
Human Immunodeficiency Virus

Enveloped +ssRNA virus.
Retrovirus (has reverse transcriptase).

## Other particles

**Viroids** - only short strand of circular RNA capable of self-replication.

**Virusoids** - non-self-replicating ssRNAs, need a helper virus to coinfect the same cell to replicate.

**Prions** - proteinaceous infections particles; misfolded proteins, causes other proteins to misfold.
Transmissible spongiform encephalopathy (TSE).
Example:
	Humans - Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease.
	Animals - mad cow syndrome
