## Protozoans

Free-living/parasitic unicellular eukaryotes.
Asexual/sexual reproduction.
Complex cell shapes.
Large variation of organelles.
Have a plasma membrane (plasmalemma).
**Endoplasm** - inner cytoplasm; "classical" cytoplasm.
**Ectoplasm** - outer cytoplasm; between 2 membranes.

Some have **cytostome** for feeding & **cytoproct** for waste excretion.

Can switch between being haploid & diploid.

#### *Excavata* \[supergrp.]

Typically w/ flagella for movement.

***Fornicata***
\[phylum]

***Giardia lamblia*** - diarrhea, cysts; spreads through water; flagella, no mitochondria or Golgi apparatus.

***Parabasalia***
\[phylum]

Animal endosymbionts, flagella.
***Trichomonas vaginalis*** - trich, STD, many asymptomatic carriers, premature labour if pregnant, no cysts; no mitochondria.

***Euglenozoa***
\[phylum]

***Kinetoplastids*** \[class] - microbes w/ modified single mitochondria w/ large mass DNA genome.
***Trypanosoma brucei*** - African sleeping sickness; tse tse fly.
***Trypanosoma cruzi*** - South American. Chagas disease; "kissing bugs" (bite face during sleep!)
***Leishmania*** - disfiguring skin disease; sand fly.

***Percolozoa***
\[phylum]

***Naegleria fowregi*** - brain-eating amoeba; from fresh water, enters through the nose.

#### Chromalveolata

***Diatoms and Dinoflagellates***
Photosynthetic.


***Apicomplexans***
\[phylum]

Were once photosynthetic, but lost the ability, obligate parasites..
**Apicoplast** - relic chloroplast.
**Apical complex** - secretes proteins & lipids for invasion.
Move by **actin-myosin** motor - propulsion.
**Schizogony** - replicate DNA a lot, then divide.
***Cryptosporidium parvum*** - diarrhea; from water.
***Theileria (Babesia) microti*** - recurring fever; transmitted by ticks.
***Babesia bovis*** - cattle pathogen.
***Toxoplasma gondii*** - toxoplasmosis, cysts in brain, birth defects, healthy asymptomatic carriers; spreads through cat litter.
***Plasmodium falciparum, Plasmodium vivax*** - malaria, cyclic fevers, neurological complications.


***Ciliates***
\[phylum]

Move by cilia
Contain **macronucleus** (polyploid, metabolism) & **micronucleus** (diploid, sexual reproduction).

#### Amoebazoa

**Amoeboid movement** - crawling using **pseudopodia** (cytoplasm-filled projections).

Entamoeba
***Entamoeba histolytica*** - amoebic dysentery, blood stools, can be asymptomatic; spreads through water.
***Acanthamoeba*** - blindness.

Eumycetozoa (Slime molds)
Unicellular organisms coming together & working together.
Form fluiting bodies w/ spores.

Dictyostelium discoideum - cellular smile molds.
Plasmodial slime molds - large cells w/ multiple nuclei.

## Fungi

**Mycosis** - infections by fungi.

Fingal structure differences:
**Chitin** - in cell wall.
**Ergosterols** - instead of cholesterol in cell membrane.

**Hyphae** - chains of fungal cells forming a mold.
**Dimorphic fungi**  - can exist as both yeast and molds.

***Saccharomyces cerevisiae*** - budding yeast, used for baking.
***Candida albicans*** - common infection, oral & genital thrush, opportunistic, part of gut microbiota.
***Candida auris*** - generally only in hospitals & care homes, infects wounds, blood, ears; **antifungal-resistant**.
***Histoplasma capsulatum*** - mold outside, yeast in human.
