
Aerobic bacteria go through the usual glycolysis → TCA cycle → ETC pipeline using $\ce{O2}$.

In prokaryotes ETC happens in the cell membrane.

Denitrifiers use $\ce{NO3-}$ and $\ce{NO2}$ as final $e^-$ acceptor creating $\ce{N2}$.
Other anaerobes use sulfate, sulfur, fumarate, $\ce{CO2}$, $\ce{Fe^{3+}}$ etc.

For fermentation, pyruvate is the final $e^-$ acceptor.
