
Lots of prokaryotes live with other organisms.

![[symbiosis.png]]

**Microbiome** - microbes associated with a higher organism.

**Sexual reproduction** - same species if can produce fertile offspring.
**Asexual reproduction** - same species if similar DNA sequences, mostly 16S rRNA gene.
Prokaryotes mainly reproduce asexually, so species are classified genetically.

No official taxonomy of prokaryotes & archaea.
"**Bergey's Manual of Systematic Bacteriology**" - definitive source of all identified species' names.
"**Taxonomic Outline of the Bacteria and Archaea**" - Michigan State University effort to classify species.

Domain - Phylum - Class - Order - Family - Genus - Species

## Gram-negative bacteria

#### Proteobacteria
\[Phylum]

Major phylum of Gram-negative bacteria.

###### *Alphaproteobacteria*
\[Class]

**Oligotrophs** - low-nutrient environments.
**Facultative intracellular pathogens** - can exist & reproduce outside the host.
**Obligative intracellular pathogens** - cannot exist nor reproduce outside the host.
	***Chlamydia trachomatis*** - different strains infect different organs (genitals, eyes, lymph nodes).
	Develops a protective vacuole and produces endospore-like elementary bodies.

***Rhizobium*** \[Genus]
Nitrogen fixating symbionts, need plant host (clover, beans, legumes).

###### *Betaproteobacteria*
\[Class]

**Eutrophs** - needs lots of nutrients.
Often between aerobic and anaerobic areas.

***Neisseria meningitidis*** – bacterial meningitis; bacteria passes the blood-brain barrier.
***Neisseria gonorrhoeae*** – gonorrhea.
***Bordetella pertussis*** – whooping cough.
***Burkholderia cepacia*** - cystic fibrosis patients, nosocomial infections.

###### *Gammaproteobacteria*
\[Class]

Diverse class, many human pathogens

***Pseudomonas*** \[Genus]
***P. aeruginosa*** - cystic fibrosis patients, wound infections, nosocomial infections, highly resistant to antibiotics.

***Haemophilus influenzae*** - meningitis, ear infections, respiratory tract infections; first completely sequenced genome (1995).

***Vibrionales*** \[Genus]
***Vibrio cholerae*** - alkaline salt water, cholera, diarrhea, dehydration, outbreaks in poor sanitation.

***Legionella*** \[Genus]
***L. pneumophila*** - Legionnaires disease (Pontiac fever), respiratory disease, water droplets in the air.

***Enterobacteriaceae*** \[Family] - large family of intestinal bacteria.
***Escherichia coli*** - urinary tract infection, Traveller's diarrhea, most studied bacteria.
***Shigella*** \[Genus] - diarrhea, dysentery, shiga toxin.
***Salmonella*** \[Genus] - diarrhea, ***S. typhi*** causes typhoid fever.
***Yersinia pestis*** - plague.

###### *Deltaproteobacteria*
\[Class]

Small class.

***Myxococcus xanthus*** - glides over surface searching for prey and kill in packs (social). forms multicellular fruiting bodies.

###### *Epsilonproteobacteria*
\[Class]

Smallest class of bacteria.
Microaerophilic.

***Campylobacter jejuni*** - most common food poisoning in Europe & USA.

#### Nonproteobacteria

Spirochetes - bacteria with long spiral shapes.

***Treponema pallidum*** - syphilis.
***Borrelia burgdorferi*** - Lyme disease spread by ticks.

***Bacteroides*** \[Genus] - obligate anaerobic, important part of human gut microbiome.

#### Phototrophic bacteria

***Prochlorococcus marinus*** - cyanobacteria, toxic algal blooms.

## Gram-positive bacteria

#### High G+C

***Actinobacteria*** \[Phylum]

Diverse appearance, habitats, genomes.

***Actinomyces*** \[Genus] - some in soil, other cause periodontitis/endocarditis.

***Mycobacterium*** \[Genus] - mycolic acid coat (blocks gram stain).
***M. tuberculosis*** - tuberculosis.
***M. leprae*** - leprosy.

***Corynebacterium*** \[Genus] - most non-pathogenic.
***C. diphtheria*** - diphtheria, toxin.
***Bifidobacterium*** \[Genus] - anaerobes, probiotic in the gut.

#### Low G+C

***Clostridia*** \[Class] - anaerobe, endospores, lots of toxins
***C. perfringens*** - food poisoning, gas gangrene.
***C. tetani*** - soil bacteria, tetanus, neurotoxin.
***C. botulinum*** - botulism neurotoxin.
***C. difficile*** - hospital-acquired infections, from antimicrobial therapy.

***Streptococcus*** \[Genus]
**S. pyogenes** - bacterial pharyngitis, flesh-eating.
**S. mutans** - dental caries.
**S. pneumoniae** - respiratory infections, meningitis, septicemia, osteomyelitis, and endocarditis.

***Bacillus*** \[Genus] - endospores.
***B. subtilis*** - fermentation; model for Gram-positive bacteria.
***B. anthracis*** - anthrax, lethal without treatment, animal → human.
***B. cereus*** - food poisoning, heat resistant toxin.
***B. thuringiensis*** - biological pesticide

***Staphylococcus*** \[Genus] - facultative anaerobic, halophilic, non-motile.
***S. epidermidis*** - human skin, pathogenic in immune-suppressed.
***S. aureus*** - skin infections, food poisoning, toxic shock syndrome, some mutants highly resistant to antibiotics.

## Deeply branching bacteria

Close to last universal common ancestor (LUCA) - clue to early life.
Would be thermophilic.

**Deinococcus radiodurans** - survives heat, drought, vacuum, acidity, radiation.

## Archaea

No peptidoglycan.
None pathogenic.
5 phyla, 2 best characterised.

#### *Crenarchaeota*

Diverse, aquatic, most abundant organisms in the ocean.
Some hyperthermophiles.

#### *Euryarchaeota*

Methanogens.
Halobacteria + photosynthetic with a pink pigment.
