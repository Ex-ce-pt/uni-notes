#Information 
Genotype → Phenotype

All the cells of an organism have the same genome, but different genes are expressed.

- 4 000 genes in *E. coli*
- 200 000 genes in humans


Genes are [[Information Pathways/Transcription|transcribed]] & translated only when they are needed.
**Housekeeping genes** - always needed.

**Transcriptome** - all mRNA inside a cell at a certain time.
**Proteome** - all proteins in a cell at a certain time.

**Quorum sensing** - sense of population density in bacteria; they use it to regulate genes.

==ways to regulate gene expression


can regulate if rna polymerase binds or not by changing the sigma factor (like a nozzle/needle for the polymerase)
rna encoding different sigma cofactors are always present in the cell


#### Negative regulation

Regulation **inhibits** transcription.

**Repressors** - proteins that block RNA Polymerase binding to or moving along the DNA.
**Operators** - binding sites on the DNA that bind repressors (usually near promoters).
**Effectors** - small molecules that regulate repressor binding to DNA (signaling molecules).

Regulation **promotes** the transcription.

**Activators** - proteins that enhance the RNA Polymerase-promoter interaction.
